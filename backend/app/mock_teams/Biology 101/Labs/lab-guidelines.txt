Biology 101 — Lab Guidelines and Safety

General rules:
- Always wear safety goggles and a lab coat; tie back long hair.
- No food or drink in the lab.
- Know the location of the eyewash station, safety shower and fire
  extinguisher.
- Report all spills and injuries to the TA immediately.

Microscope use:
- Start on the lowest objective; use coarse focus only at low power.
- Total magnification = objective x ocular (e.g. 40x x 10x = 400x).
- Prepare wet mounts with a drop of water and a coverslip at 45 degrees.

Dissection and specimens:
- Use gloves when handling preserved specimens.
- Dispose of sharps in the sharps container, never the trash.

Lab reports: include hypothesis, methods, results (tables/graphs),
discussion and conclusion. Graphs need labeled axes with units.
