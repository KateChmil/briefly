Genetics Overview — Lecture Notes

DNA structure: double helix, sugar-phosphate backbone, complementary base
pairing (A-T, G-C). Chargaff's rules. Replication is semi-conservative;
key enzymes: helicase, primase, DNA polymerase (5'->3'), ligase.

Central dogma: DNA -> RNA -> protein.
Transcription: RNA polymerase synthesizes mRNA from template strand;
in eukaryotes, pre-mRNA is processed (5' cap, poly-A tail, splicing of
introns). Translation: ribosomes read codons; tRNA delivers amino acids;
start codon AUG (Met), stop codons UAA, UAG, UGA.

Mendelian genetics: law of segregation and independent assortment.
Dominant vs recessive alleles; genotype vs phenotype; Punnett squares.
Monohybrid cross ratio 3:1; dihybrid 9:3:3:1 (unlinked genes).

Extensions: incomplete dominance, codominance (ABO blood groups),
linked genes, sex-linked traits (X-linked recessive more common in males),
polygenic inheritance.

Mutations: point mutations (silent, missense, nonsense), frameshift
(insertions/deletions), chromosomal mutations. Causes: replication errors,
mutagens (UV, chemicals).

Population genetics: Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium (p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1);
conditions: no mutation, random mating, no selection, large population,
no gene flow.
