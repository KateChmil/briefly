Lab Safety Rules

- Goggles and closed shoes at all times; tie back long hair.
- No eating or drinking in the lab.
- Report spills and breakage immediately; never pick up glass by hand.
- Label every sample with name, date and contents.
- Osmosis practical: handle dialysis tubing gently, it tears easily.
- Wash hands before leaving; return microscopes with the low-power
  objective in place.
