Integration Techniques - Summary

u-substitution: reverse of the chain rule; pick u = inner function.
By parts: int u dv = uv - int v du; choose u by LIATE.
Trig identities: sin^2 + cos^2 = 1, half-angle formulas for even powers.
Trig substitution: a^2 - x^2 -> x = a sin(t); a^2 + x^2 -> x = a tan(t).
Partial fractions: factor the denominator, split into linear/quadratic
terms, solve for the constants.

Improper integrals: take limits at infinity or at discontinuities;
compare with p-integrals for convergence.
