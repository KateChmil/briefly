Series and Sequences - Convergence Cheatsheet

Geometric series: sum of r^n converges iff |r| < 1, to 1/(1-r).
p-series: sum 1/n^p converges iff p > 1 (harmonic p=1 diverges).

Tests:
- nth term test: if a_n does not tend to 0, the series diverges.
- Ratio test: lim |a_{n+1}/a_n| < 1 converges, > 1 diverges.
- Root test: lim |a_n|^(1/n) same thresholds.
- Comparison / limit comparison against a known series.
- Integral test for positive decreasing terms.
- Alternating series: decreasing terms to 0 -> converges; check absolute
  vs conditional convergence.

Taylor: e^x, sin, cos, ln(1+x) expansions; radius of convergence via the
ratio test.
